# What is the book about as a whole?

The book is about a community (descriptions are vague in the book) with towns where objects are disappearing. They don't vanished, the context of the object disappear, and people have to burn them, or don't pay attention to them (such as birds). Though some people don't forget. A Memory Police is in charge to make sure people do forget.

# What are the 3-4 central questions it tries to answer?

Not questions but observations:

1. Things are vanishing without protest.
2. We don't know why some people don't forget, nor why things are disappearing. It just does.
3. The crack down of the Memory Police shows that more people remembers than it would appear.
4. The author/main character manages to remember to write even if "novels" disappeared.
